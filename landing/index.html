Everything you need

Not an app. A companion.

Six features built around the real daily life of an Ethiopian patient, not a Silicon Valley health metric.

Glucose logging

Log up to 6 readings a day for fasting, post-meal, and bedtime. Know exactly what your injera and shiro does to your blood sugar, in numbers.

AI daily tips

Not generic health advice. A story-driven tip every day, written by AI based on your actual readings, medication adherence, and habits. In Amharic or English.

Medication reminders

Never miss a Metformin dose again, even when life gets busy. Set custom times and get gentle nudges with a nightly adherence check-in.

Appointment tracking

Add your follow-up dates and screening appointments. Get reminders 1 week before and 1 day before, so you never miss a check-up.

Symptom monitoring

Log thirst, fatigue, headaches, vision changes, and more. The app watches for patterns and alerts you when your symptoms correlate with your glucose readings.

Doctor report

One tap generates a clean PDF with 90 days of data, including glucose trends, medication adherence, and symptoms. Hand it to your doctor and cut the guesswork out of every visit.

Is Tenachin AI free to use?
Yes, completely free. There are no paid tiers, no subscriptions, and no hidden fees. Tenachin AI is free to download and use for all Ethiopian patients.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. The app saves all your readings locally first. When your internet connection returns, everything syncs automatically. You never lose a reading because of poor connectivity.
Is it available in Amharic?
Yes. Amharic is a first-class language in Tenachin AI, not a translation. You can switch between Amharic and English any time in settings. AI tips are generated directly in your chosen language.
Does it replace my doctor?
No, and it's designed not to. Tenachin AI helps you track, learn, and remember. Your doctor makes the clinical decisions. The PDF report you generate is meant to make your doctor's time more productive, not to replace them.
What phones does it work on?
Tenachin AI works on Android (5.0 and above) and iPhone (iOS 13 and above). For Android users without Google Play access, you can download the APK directly from this page.
Who built this app?
Tenachin AI was built by an Ethiopian developer designed specifically for the Ethiopian healthcare reality including local diets and medications available in Ethiopia.
